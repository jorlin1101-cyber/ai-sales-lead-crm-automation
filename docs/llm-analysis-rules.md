## 1. Purpose

本文档定义 AI Sales Automation System 中 LLM lead analysis 的输出规则。

当前系统采用：

```text
LLM-first + rule-based fallback
```

也就是说：

* 当 LLM 可用时，优先使用 LLM 做复杂语义判断。
* 当 LLM 不可用、超时、输出不合法或无法通过 Pydantic 校验时，系统回退到 rule-based scoring。
* LLM 输出不能直接写入 CRM，必须先经过 `LeadAnalysisResult` 校验。

---

## 2. Input

LLM analysis 接收的是 `CleanedLead`。

主要字段：

* `name`
* `email`
* `company_name`
* `message`
* `source`

进入 LLM analysis 前，lead 已经完成：

* Pydantic 输入校验
* 字段清洗
* email 小写化
* source 默认值处理
* business validation

无效 lead 不应该进入 LLM analysis。

---

## 3. Output Schema

LLM 必须输出符合 `LeadAnalysisResult` 的结构。

字段包括：

* `lead_type`
* `lead_subtype`
* `intent_level`
* `lead_score`
* `lead_summary`
* `recommended_action`
* `followup_email_draft`
* `analysis_method`
* `confidence`

---

## 4. Structured Fields

以下字段必须严格使用固定值，不能自由发挥。

### 4.1 lead_type

Allowed values:

```text
B2B
B2C
Unknown
```

### 4.2 lead_subtype

Allowed values:

```text
Agency
Operator
School
Corporate
Influencer
LargeGroup

LuxuryHighBudget
FIT
Other
Unknown
```

### 4.3 intent_level

Allowed values:

```text
High
Medium
Low
Unknown
```

### 4.4 analysis_method

Allowed values:

```text
llm
rule_fallback
```

When the result is generated by LLM, use:

```text
analysis_method = "llm"
```

When the result is generated by rule-based fallback, use:

```text
analysis_method = "rule_fallback"
```

### 4.5 lead_score

Allowed range:

```text
0–100
```

### 4.6 confidence

Allowed range:

```text
0–1
```

Meaning:

```text
0 = no confidence
1 = very high confidence
```

## 5. Natural Language Fields

以下字段允许使用自然语言，但必须清晰、简洁、可供人工审核。

### 5.1 lead_summary

Purpose:

```text
总结这条 lead 是谁、想要什么、为什么有价值。
```

Requirements:

* Must not be empty.
* Should be 1–3 sentences.
* Should not invent information not present in the lead.
* Should mention important business signals when available.

Example:

```text
A Spanish travel agency is asking for a quotation for a 20-person private China tour in September. The lead shows strong intent and clear group travel value.
```

---

### 5.2 recommended_action

Purpose:

```text
给销售人员下一步处理建议。
```

Requirements:

* Must not be empty.
* Should be actionable.
* Should match the intent level and lead subtype.
* Should not recommend sending automatically without human review.

Example:

```text
Review immediately and prepare a tailored follow-up asking for travel dates, budget range, and preferred destinations.
```

---

### 5.3 followup_email_draft

Purpose:

```text
生成一封可人工修改的跟进邮件草稿。
```

Requirements when `analysis_method = "llm"`:

* Should be polite and professional.
* Should reference the user's inquiry.
* Should ask for missing key details when needed.
* Should not promise unavailable services.
* Should not be sent automatically without human review.

Requirements when `analysis_method = "rule_fallback"`:

* May be an empty string.
* Rule fallback is not required to generate a full email draft.

## 6. Lead Type Rules

### 6.1 B2B

Use `B2B` when the lead appears to come from an organization or business partnership context.

Examples:

* travel agency
* tour operator
* school
* university
* corporate travel
* influencer / creator collaboration
* business partnership

Possible subtypes:

```text
Agency
Operator
School
Corporate
Influencer
Other
```

---

### 6.2 B2C

Use `B2C` when the lead appears to come from an individual traveler, family, couple, private group, or personal travel inquiry.

Examples:

* family trip
* couple trip
* private custom tour
* large group of friends or family
* luxury private travel
* independent traveler

Possible subtypes:

```text
LargeGroup
PrivateCustom
LuxuryHighBudget
FIT
Other
```

---

### 6.3 Unknown

Use `Unknown` only when the message does not provide enough information to classify the lead.

Do not overuse `Unknown`.

If the lead clearly indicates a personal traveler, classify it as B2C rather than Unknown.

---

## 7. Intent Level Rules

### 7.1 High

Use `High` when the lead shows clear buying, booking, quotation, partnership, or planning intent.

Signals:

* asks for quotation
* wants to book
* mentions dates
* mentions group size
* asks for private or custom tour
* mentions budget
* partnership or collaboration request
* clear destination and travel plan

---

### 7.2 Medium

Use `Medium` when the lead shows interest but lacks concrete planning details.

Signals:

* asks for more information
* asks if something is possible
* asks general questions
* shows interest but no dates, group size, budget, or destination

---

### 7.3 Low

Use `Low` when the lead is vague, weak, or not ready for sales follow-up.

Signals:

* very short message
* no clear request
* no planning details
* unclear travel intent

---

## 8. Lead Score Guidance

LLM should assign `lead_score` based on:

```text
customer fit
+ intent strength
+ order value potential
+ information completeness
```

Score ranges:

```text
75–100 = High
45–74 = Medium
0–44 = Low
```

The `intent_level` should match the score range.

Examples:

* A travel agency asking for a quotation for a 20-person China tour should usually be High.
* A family asking vaguely about traveling to China may be Low or Medium depending on details.
* A luxury private custom tour inquiry may be High even if it is B2C.
* An invalid or unclear inquiry should not be over-scored.

---

## 9. Fallback Conditions

The system should fallback to rule-based scoring when:

* LLM API is unavailable
* API key is missing
* request times out
* model output is not valid JSON
* model output fails `LeadAnalysisResult` validation
* required fields are missing
* enum values are invalid
* confidence is outside 0–1
* lead_score is outside 0–100

Fallback result should still return a valid `LeadAnalysisResult`.

---

## 10. Safety and Quality Rules

LLM must not:

* invent missing facts
* claim a budget exists if not provided
* claim a booking is confirmed if not stated
* generate aggressive sales language
* send emails automatically
* ignore validation status
* output fields outside the schema

LLM should:

* be conservative when information is missing
* ask for missing details
* clearly summarize the lead
* recommend human review before follow-up
* produce structured output only

## 8. Lead Score Guidance

LLM should assign `lead_score` based on the lead's overall business value, not only keyword matching.

The score should consider four dimensions:

```text
customer fit
+ intent strength
+ order value potential
+ information completeness
```

### 8.1 Customer Fit

Evaluate whether the lead matches the business target.

High-value customer types include:

* B2B travel agency
* B2B tour operator
* school or university group
* corporate / incentive travel
* influencer or content collaboration
* B2C large group
* B2C private custom tour
* B2C luxury or high-budget traveler

Do not automatically treat all B2C leads as low value.
A large group, private custom tour, or luxury B2C inquiry can be high value.

### 8.2 Intent Strength

Evaluate whether the lead shows clear buying, booking, quotation, planning, or partnership intent.

High intent signals include:

* asking for a quotation
* requesting a booking
* mentioning specific travel dates
* mentioning group size
* asking for a private or custom itinerary
* discussing budget
* proposing partnership or collaboration

Medium intent signals include:

* asking for general information
* asking if something is possible
* showing interest without concrete plans

Low intent signals include:

* vague message
* no clear travel request
* no dates, group size, destination, or budget
* unclear business value

### 8.3 Order Value Potential

Evaluate whether the lead may bring meaningful commercial value.

High-value signals include:

* large group
* private tour
* custom itinerary
* luxury or premium service
* multi-day trip
* high budget
* long-term partnership
* specific destination and travel plan

Medium-value signals include:

* family trip
* couple trip
* private guide
* custom tour
* itinerary inquiry

Low-value signals include:

* very general inquiry
* no planning details
* no commercial signal

### 8.4 Information Completeness

Evaluate whether the lead provides enough useful information for sales follow-up.

Useful information includes:

* company name
* group size
* travel date or month
* destination
* budget
* travel style
* itinerary needs
* accommodation preference
* source of inquiry

More complete leads should usually receive a higher score than vague leads.

### 8.5 Score Ranges

Use the following score ranges:

```text
75–100 = High intent / high priority
45–74  = Medium intent / needs review
0–44   = Low intent / weak or unclear lead
```

The `intent_level` must match the score range:

```text
High   → lead_score must be 75–100
Medium → lead_score must be 45–74
Low    → lead_score must be 0–44
```

Do not output inconsistent results such as:

```text
intent_level = "High"
lead_score = 40
```

### 8.6 Scoring Behavior

LLM should not rely only on exact keywords.

For example:

* A travel agency asking for a 20-person quotation should usually be High.
* A B2C family group of 12 people asking for a private custom tour can also be High.
* A luxury private tour inquiry can be High even if it is B2C.
* A vague message like “I want to travel” should usually be Low or Medium.
* If information is missing, reduce confidence and avoid over-scoring.

When uncertain, choose a conservative score and explain the uncertainty in `lead_summary` or `recommended_action`.
